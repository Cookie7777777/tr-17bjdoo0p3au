Methodology

plain words

1 · Discovery

About every 30 minutes while the scanner is online (roughly 08:30–21:00 PT), it pulls new pools on Solana, BSC and Robinhood chain with at least $50k liquidity that were created in the last 24h (CoinGecko on-chain megafilter plus GeckoTerminal new pools). Every pool is re-reviewed on each scan during its first 24 hours, using live DexScreener data.

2 · Hard skips

A pool is dropped before any rule is checked if:

  • Liquidity is under $50k, or the pair is older than 24h, or no DexScreener pair exists
  • No trades in the last hour
  • 24h price change is over +1000%
  • FDV is below LP
  • Average trade size in the last hour is under $12 (dust/bot noise)
  • Painted clone: 50+ trades in 24h but sells under 10% of them
  • Contract is on the tracker blacklist, or the creator wallet is on the deployer blacklist
  • RugCheck flags creator rug history or a copycat token; honeypot / cannot-sell flags
  • Top 10 holders own over 40% (excluding pool and AMM accounts)

3 · Rules

Rule P logs a hit when LP ≥ $50k, FDV ≥ max($300k, LP), 1h change ≤ +50% and 24h change ≤ +1000%.

Rule V logs a hit on "real volume": LP ≥ $120k, FDV ≤ 25× 1h volume, 1h volume under 5× LP, last-5-minute pace at least half the hourly rate, 1h change above −15%, and no wash-trading pattern. V was rejected for live use; it stays on this page so the comparison is visible.

P-tweaked adds pool age ≥ 60 min and sells ≥ 35% of 24h transactions.

4 · Auto-excluded junk

Hits are always logged, but these are tagged excluded and left out of every rate: LP ≥ $5M on a pool under 24h old, fake LP farms (LP ≈ FDV at $1M+ with under 100 holders), stablecoin pools, misread pools (huge LP, almost no volume), dead pools (1h volume under $2k and 24h under $10k), feed misprints (−99% entry change), an old token on a new pool, and a known shared deployer that produces misread pools. Some early rows were excluded by hand for the same reasons, or because outage fallback data skipped a gate.

5 · Outcome labels

Each hit is rechecked about 1h (45–150 min), 6h (4.5–9 h) and 24h (20–36 h) after it was logged, against the entry snapshot:

  • Rug pair gone, LP under $10k, or FDV down 90%+
  • Survivor LP ≥ $50k and FDV ≥ 50% of entry
  • Winner a survivor at ≥ 2× entry FDV
  • Faded anything else (alive but bled out)

The headline uses each hit's latest label. If the data feed is down, a recheck is deferred, never scored as a rug.

6 · Known limits

  • Small samples, a few days long. Rates will move.
  • Rechecks only happen while the scanner runs, so some horizons are missing for overnight hits (6h has the fewest checks).
  • A dead pool with a flat price can still score Survivor; known junk classes are excluded, others may slip through.
  • Holder and lock data is much better on Solana than on BSC and Robinhood.
  • Skipped pools are never rechecked, so this page can't show how many rugs each hard skip avoided.
  • Outcomes are FDV snapshots, not fills. Slippage, fees and exits are not modeled for scanner hits.
